IPC Manufacturing Standards

These globally accepted standards assure all PCBs meet strict requirements for durability, performance, and long-term reliability.

IPC-A-600

Acceptability of Printed Boards. Ensures bare boards meet strict cosmetic and physical requirements prior to assembly.

IPC-SM-840

Qualification and Performance of Permanent Solder Mask. Verifies ultimate environmental and physical mask protection.

IPC-TM-650

Test Methods Manual. Standardized procedures for evaluating electrical, mechanical, and environmental tests.

IPC-6012

Qualification and Performance Specification for Rigid PCBs. Guides structurally accurate rigid board performance.
