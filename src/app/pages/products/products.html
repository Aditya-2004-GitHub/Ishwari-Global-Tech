PCB Technologies Offered

Engineered for Your Application

Single-Sided PCBs

Contains one conductive copper layer. Cost-effective and perfectly suitable for high-volume production operations.

Applications: Consumer electronics, Power supplies, LED lighting drivers

Double-Sided PCBs

Contains copper layers on both sides of the board, allowing more complex designs and improved electrical connectivity.

Applications: Industrial equipment, Automotive, Power electronics

Multilayer PCBs

Consists of multiple conductive layers stacked together. Provides higher circuit density and better overall electrical performance.

Applications: Telecom, Automation, IoT devices, Advanced electronics

Metal Core PCBs

Uses a metal base layer to improve heat dissipation. Ideal for applications requiring extremely efficient thermal management.

Applications: High-power LED systems, Power electronics, Lighting

Flexible PCBs

Designed to naturally bend or flex during installation. Helps significantly reduce weight and save space in end products.

Applications: Wearables, Compact electronic devices, Medical devices

Rigid-Flex PCBs

Combines rigid and flexible circuit technologies. Provides high reliability in critical, compact electronic systems.

Applications: Aerospace, Medical devices, Industrial control systems

RF / High Frequency PCBs

Designed directly for specific applications requiring completely stable signal transmission and high-frequency performance.

Applications: Telecommunication, Wireless devices, Radar systems, IoT
